Adopt

Puppies draw crowds at events. A little prep makes the day smoother for you and our volunteers.

Before an event

  1. Submit your adoption application.
    Send it before the event so we can review it ahead of time.
  2. Send vet records for your current pets.
    Include them with your application.
  3. Come meet us and be patient.
    Our volunteers and staff will get to everyone.

Adopting a cat?

Adult cats are $125. That includes spay/neuter, microchip, vet exam, fecal test, FeLV/FIV test, and current FVRCP and rabies vaccines.

Cats have their own application, so please use the cat form instead of the dog one.
