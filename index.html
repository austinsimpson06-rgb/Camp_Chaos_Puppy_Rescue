Adopt

Puppies draw crowds at events. A little prep makes the day smoother for you and our volunteers.

Before an event

  1. Submit your adoption application.
    Send it before the event so we can review it ahead of time.
  2. Send vet records for your current pets.
    Include them with your application.
  3. Come meet us and be patient.
    Our volunteers and staff will get to everyone.

Full adoption info and fees

Adopting a cat?

Adult cats are $125. That includes spay/neuter, microchip, vet exam, fecal test, FeLV/FIV test, and current FVRCP and rabies vaccines.

Cats have their own application, so please use the cat form instead of the dog one.

Adoption info

Camp Chaos is a small, foster-based rescue. We take in animals from rural and local high-kill shelters, with a special focus on pregnant moms and litters of puppies.

How adoption works

  1. Explore our available animals.
    Our online listings update as animals are adopted or new ones arrive. See the listings.
  2. Visit an adoption event.
    Meet the animal you like with one of our volunteers. Everyone in your household should meet them before the adoption is final, so we know it is a good fit.
  3. Complete an adoption application.
    Applications help us match each animal with the right home. Once yours is approved, we share the animal’s medical and behavior information with you and collect the fees. You can apply before an event, but it does not hold an animal for you.
  4. Welcome your new pet home.
    Stay in touch! We are happy to help with training, health, or behavior questions, or just to hear how they are doing.
Dog applicationCat application

Adoption fee: $300

There may be small processing fees on top of that. The fee helps cover:

  • Spay/neuter
  • Microchip
  • Deworming
  • Fecal testing
  • Veterinary health exam
  • Heartworm testing
  • Age-appropriate vaccines: distemper/parvo combo, bordetella, and rabies

Adult cats are $125 instead.

Questions? Email campchaospuppyrescue@outlook.com.
